Privacy Policy

Your tabs stay on your device

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Link Collector does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any of your data. Everything it does happens inside your browser, and nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the extension reads

When you open the Link Collector popup, it reads the titles and URLs of your open tabs so it can list, filter and copy them. This information is only used to show the list in the popup. It is not saved, logged or transmitted, and it is gone when the popup closes.

What the extension saves

Link Collector saves your settings using the browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) so they are remembered next time:

  • Whether to show the current window only
  • Whether to include the active tab
  • Whether to hide duplicate URLs
  • Your exclude patterns
  • Your chosen view (cards or text) and output format

These settings stay on your computer. They are not synced to your Google account and are removed when you uninstall the extension.

What the extension does not do

  • It makes no network requests and has no servers.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • It does not read the content of web pages.
  • It does not record your browsing history.
  • It does not sell, transfer or share data with third parties.

Clipboard and opening tabs

Links are copied to your clipboard only when you click Copy or press Ctrl+Enter. Tabs are opened only when you click Open all, and only for the links shown in the text box.

Permissions

  • tabs: to read the titles and URLs of open tabs, switch to a tab when you click it, and open tabs with Open all.
  • favicon: to show each site's icon from the browser's own icon cache.
  • storage: to remember your settings on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
